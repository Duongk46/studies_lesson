IELTS Reading — Chiến Lược

SKIM → QUESTIONS → SCAN (không đọc toàn bộ passage) Bước 1 — SKIM (2-3 phút/passage): Đọc title, subtitles, first sentence mỗi đoạn → hiểu structure KHÔNG đọc chi tiết ở bước này Bước 2 — ĐỌC QUESTIONS TRƯỚC: Gạch chân keywords trong question Tìm paraphrase của keywords đó trong passage Bước 3 — SCAN để tìm đáp án: Đừng đọc lại từ đầu — đi thẳng đến vùng chứa answer TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN: TRUE = thông tin trong bài xác nhận statement FALSE = thông tin trong bài mâu thuẫn statement NG = bài không đề cập → KHÔNG SUY LUẬN Lỗi phổ biến: ❌ Đọc toàn bộ passage rồi mới nhìn câu hỏi (lãng phí 15 phút) ❌ Chọn NG khi không chắc (False và NG khác nhau hoàn toàn) ❌ Paraphrase trap: "increase" ≠ "doubled" (kiểm tra kỹ)

IELTS Listening — Chiến Lược

PREDICT → LISTEN → TRANSFER (cẩn thận spelling) Trước khi nghe (30 giây/section): Đọc trước questions → predict loại thông tin cần nghe (number? name? date? place? reason?) Chú ý LIMIT: "write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" Trong khi nghe: Nghe signal words: "however", "but", "actually" → thông tin sắp thay đổi Khi họ repeat/correct: "I mean...", "Sorry, that's..." → đáp án mới Section 1-2: monologue/dialogue casual → dễ hơn Section 3-4: academic discussion/lecture → khó hơn Sau khi nghe (10 phút transfer): Kiểm tra spelling, singular/plural, grammar fit Nếu bỏ 1 câu → ĐỪNG PANIC, tiếp tục section tiếp Bẫy thường gặp: "The meeting is on Tuesday... actually, Wednesday" → đáp án là Wednesday Số điện thoại: đọc lại lần hai thường chậm hơn → nghe lần đó Names: luôn hỏi "Could you spell that?" trong Section 1

IELTS Writing Task 2 — 5 Templates (Band 7+)

Template 1 — Opinion / Argumentative (Agree or Disagree)

Intro: "[Paraphrase topic]. While some argue that [opposing view], this essay will argue that [your position] for two main reasons: [reason 1] and [reason 2]."

Body 1: "The primary reason [position] is [Reason 1]. [Explain]. For instance, [specific example]. This demonstrates that [link back to thesis]."

Body 2: "Furthermore, [Reason 2]. [Explain]. To illustrate, [example]. Consequently, [impact]."

Conclusion: "In conclusion, [restate position] because [Reason 1] and [Reason 2]. Governments/individuals/society should therefore [recommendation]."

Template 2 — Discussion (Both Views + Opinion)

Intro: "[Paraphrase topic]. While some people believe [View A], others argue [View B]. This essay will examine both perspectives before concluding that [your view]."

Body 1 (View A): "Those who support [View A] argue that [reason]. For example, [example]. As a result, [outcome]."

Body 2 (View B + your stance): "On the other hand, proponents of [View B] contend that [reason]. To illustrate, [example]. In my view, [View B] is more compelling because [reason]."

Conclusion: "In conclusion, although [View A] has merit, [View B] is ultimately more persuasive. Therefore, [recommendation]."

Template 3 — Problem / Solution

Intro: "[Paraphrase topic] is an increasingly pressing issue in modern society. This essay will examine the main causes of this problem and propose effective solutions."

Body 1 (Problems/Causes): "The principal cause of [issue] is [Cause 1]. [Explain + example]. Additionally, [Cause 2] plays a significant role. [Explain + example]."

Body 2 (Solutions): "To address [Cause 1], [Solution 1] should be implemented. [Explain]. Furthermore, [Solution 2] would effectively tackle [Cause 2]. [Explain + evidence]."

Conclusion: "In conclusion, [issue] stems primarily from [causes]. By implementing [solutions], [positive outcome] can be achieved."

Template 4 — Advantages / Disadvantages

Intro: "[Paraphrase topic]. This essay will analyse the advantages and disadvantages of this trend before reaching a conclusion."

Body 1 (Advantages): "The most significant advantage of [X] is [Advantage 1]. [Explain + example]. Moreover, [Advantage 2]. [Explain + example]."

Body 2 (Disadvantages): "However, [X] is not without its drawbacks. Chief among these is [Disadvantage 1]. [Explain + example]. In addition, [Disadvantage 2]. [Explain + example]."

Conclusion: "In conclusion, while [X] offers [advantages], the disadvantages — particularly [main disadvantage] — suggest that a more cautious approach is warranted."

Template 5 — Cause / Effect (+ Solutions)

Intro: "[Paraphrase topic] has become a significant challenge in recent decades. This essay will explore the primary causes of this phenomenon and its consequent effects."

Body 1 (Causes): "The primary factor contributing to [issue] is [Cause 1], which occurs because [explanation]. A further cause is [Cause 2], driven by [explanation + example]."

Body 2 (Effects): "This has led to a range of significant effects. Most notably, [Effect 1]. [Explain + data if possible]. Furthermore, [Effect 2], which results in [consequence]."

Conclusion: "In conclusion, [issue] is caused primarily by [causes], resulting in [effects]. Unless [action] is taken, [negative projection]."

IELTS Writing Task 1 — Graph/Chart Language

CẤU TRÚC BẮT BUỘC: Introduction (1-2 câu) → Overview (2 câu) → Body 1 → Body 2 OVERVIEW LÀ BẮT BUỘC — thiếu overview = tối đa Band 5 "Overall, it is clear that [main trend 1] while [main trend 2]." "In general, [key feature 1], with [key feature 2] being most notable." MỞ ĐẦU (paraphrase đề, đừng copy): "The [bar chart/line graph/pie chart/table] illustrates/shows/depicts..." "The [diagram] compares/presents/provides information about..." TỪ CHỈ XU HƯỚNG TĂNG: rise, increase, grow, climb, surge, soar, rocket, jump, shoot up (+ adverbs: sharply, dramatically, significantly, gradually, steadily, slightly) TỪ CHỈ XU HƯỚNG GIẢM: fall, decrease, decline, drop, plummet, slump, dip, tumble ỔN ĐỊNH / BIẾN ĐỘNG: remain stable/constant, level off, plateau, stay at → ổn định fluctuate, vary, be erratic, oscillate → biến động CHỈ SỐ (dùng để mô tả data): approximately, roughly, around, about, just over/under, nearly, almost exactly, precisely → khi biết chính xác more than double, triple, quadruple → khi tăng nhiều lần

IELTS Speaking — Chiến Lược 3 Parts

PART 1 — Personal questions (4-5 phút) Câu hỏi về: hometown, work/study, hobbies, daily routine, food, travel... Câu trả lời: 2-4 câu (không quá dài, không quá ngắn) Template Part 1: [Direct answer] + [Reason/Detail] + [Personal example] "Yes, I really enjoy [X]. Mainly because [reason]. For instance, [example]." PART 2 — Cue card monologue (1 phút chuẩn bị + 2 phút nói) Dùng 1 phút chuẩn bị để ghi bullet points, KHÔNG viết đầy đủ câu Nói đủ 2 phút — nếu hết ý thì mở rộng sang feelings, reflection, comparison Template Part 2 (PEEL-extended): "I'm going to talk about [topic]." [When/Where]: "This was/happened [time + place]..." [What]: "What I specifically remember is..." [Who]: "The [person/people] involved were..." [Why memorable]: "The reason this stands out to me is..." [Reflection]: "Looking back, I think/realize..." PART 3 — Abstract discussion (4-5 phút) Câu hỏi mở, học thuật hơn — đòi hỏi opinions + reasoning Dùng: hedging, discourse markers, examples Template Part 3 phrases: "That's an interesting question. I think..." "It really depends on [factor]. For instance..." "From a societal perspective..." "Research suggests that..." "I'd argue that [position], primarily because [reason]." "There are different schools of thought on this..." "On one hand... on the other hand..." LỖI THƯỜNG GẶP TRONG SPEAKING: ❌ Câu trả lời 1 từ: "Yes." / "No." → luôn elaborate ❌ Học thuộc lòng → nghe giả tạo, examiner sẽ hỏi off-script ❌ Pause im lặng dài → dùng fillers: "That's a good question..." ❌ Nói quá nhanh → slow down, pausing shows confidence

Writing Band 7 Checklist

❌ Band 5-6 Writing
  • Simple sentences only
  • Repetitive vocabulary (good, bad, big)
  • No cohesive devices
  • Off-topic paragraphs
  • No overview (Task 1)
  • Under 250 words (Task 2)
  • Informal language (gonna, wanna)
✓ Band 7+ Writing
  • Mix simple + complex sentences
  • Varied precise vocabulary (AWL words)
  • Cohesive devices used naturally
  • Every paragraph linked to thesis
  • Clear overview (Task 1)
  • 260-290 words (Task 2 sweet spot)
  • Formal academic register throughout

TOEFL Writing — Integrated & Independent Templates

Task 1 — Integrated Writing (Reading + Lecture, 20 phút, 150-225 words)

Intro: "The reading passage argues that [main claim]. However, the lecturer challenges this view, contending that [counter-claim]."

Point 1: "First, while the reading states that [point from reading], the lecturer counters this by explaining that [counter-point]. Specifically, [detail from lecture]."

Point 2: "Second, the reading claims [point 2]. The professor disputes this, noting that [counter-point 2]. According to the lecture, [detail]."

Point 3: "Finally, regarding [point 3 from reading], the lecturer argues that [counter]. This undermines the reading's assertion that [restate reading point]."

Task 2 — Academic Discussion (10 phút, 100+ words)

Structure: Agree/disagree + reason + example OR Build on someone else's point

"I agree with [Student A/B]'s perspective that [point]. [Your expanded reason]. For example, [specific example]. Furthermore, [additional point that adds value to the discussion]."

Key: Respond to the discussion prompt AND engage with at least one classmate's post. Show critical thinking, not just restating opinions.

TOEFL Speaking — 4 Tasks

Task 1 — Independent (15s prep, 45s speak) Topic: Personal preference / opinion Template: "Personally, I prefer [X] for two reasons. First, [reason 1 + example]. Second, [reason 2 + example]. For these reasons, I prefer [X]." Task 2 — Campus Situation (30s prep, 60s speak) Read announcement (45s) → Listen to conversation → Speak Template: "The [man/woman] [agrees/disagrees] with the [policy/plan]. First, [reason 1 from conversation + detail]. Additionally, [reason 2 + detail]." Task 3 — Academic Content (30s prep, 60s speak) Read definition (45s) → Listen to lecture example → Explain Template: "The professor explains [concept] using the example of [X]. [Describe what happened]. This illustrates [concept] because [reason]." Task 4 — Academic Lecture (20s prep, 60s speak) Listen to lecture → Summarize using examples Template: "The professor discusses [topic], explaining that [main point]. To illustrate, [example 1]. Furthermore, [example 2]. These examples demonstrate [key concept]."

Use of English — Key Grammar Parts

PART 1 — Multiple Choice Cloze (vocabulary + collocation) Test: collocations, phrasal verbs, fixed expressions, idioms Strategy: Đọc cả câu/đoạn trước khi chọn → không chọn theo cảm giác PART 2 — Open Cloze (grammar words) Điền: prepositions, articles, pronouns, conjunctions, auxiliary verbs No options given — phải biết grammar tốt Common: although/despite, despite/in spite of, such/so, much/many PART 3 — Word Formation Cho: base word → điền dạng đúng (noun/verb/adj/adv + negative prefix) Ví dụ: EMPLOY → employment / employer / unemployed / employable Phải biết: prefix (un-, in-, dis-, mis-, over-) + suffix (-tion, -ment, -ful, -less, -ity) PART 4 — Key Word Transformation (đặc thù Cambridge) Rewrite câu dùng từ cho sẵn, không thay đổi nghĩa, dùng 2-5 từ Ví dụ: "It was difficult to understand him." → DIFFICULTY Answer: "I had difficulty understanding him." Test: conditionals, passive, reported speech, comparatives, phrasal verbs
Phần 6 — Academic Vocabulary

Academic Word List (AWL) — Coxhead (2000)

570 từ gia đình chiếm ~10% từ vựng trong văn bản học thuật. Thiết yếu cho IELTS Academic, TOEFL, Cambridge. Tổ chức theo chức năng, không theo alphabet.

📚
Cách học AWL hiệu quả nhất (theo Coxhead)

Học theo word families (employ → employment, employer, employee, unemployed). Mỗi lần gặp từ, tìm 3 từ trong cùng family. Học trong context, không học danh sách naked words.

Nhóm 1: Analysis & Evaluation

analyze / analysis / analytical → "The study analyzes the impact of..." assess / assessment / assessable → "We must assess the risks before..." evaluate / evaluation / evaluative → "Researchers evaluated the outcomes..." examine / examination → "This essay examines three factors..." investigate / investigation → "Scientists investigated the correlation..." interpret / interpretation → "The data can be interpreted as..." identify / identification → "The study identified three key issues..." indicate / indication → "Results indicate a significant trend..." demonstrate / demonstration → "This demonstrates that..." illustrate / illustration → "To illustrate this point..."

Nhóm 2: Argumentation & Claims

argue / argument / arguable / arguably → "Some argue that technology..." assert / assertion → "The author asserts that..." claim / claim (n) → "Critics claim this approach is flawed..." contend / contention → "Researchers contend that..." maintain / maintenance → "He maintains that the policy is effective..." propose / proposal → "This essay proposes three solutions..." suggest / suggestion → "The evidence suggests that..." imply / implication → "This implies a causal relationship..." conclude / conclusion → "It can be concluded that..." acknowledge / acknowledgement → "Researchers acknowledge the limitations..."

Nhóm 3: Change & Process

alter / alteration → "Climate change has altered ecosystems..." modify / modification → "The policy was modified to include..." transform / transformation → "Technology has transformed education..." shift / shift (n) → "A paradigm shift has occurred in..." implement / implementation → "The government implemented new regulations..." establish / establishment → "The study establishes a clear link..." generate / generation → "This approach generates significant revenue..." facilitate / facilitation → "Technology facilitates communication..." constitute / constitution → "These factors constitute a significant risk..." derive / derivation → "Income derived from tourism..."

Nhóm 4: Research & Data

data (always plural in academic: "data are...") evidence / evident / evidently → "There is strong evidence to suggest..." findings → "The findings reveal that..." statistics / statistical → "Statistical analysis shows..." method / methodology → "A qualitative methodology was adopted..." survey / survey (v) → "A survey of 500 participants was conducted..." hypothesis / hypothesize → "The hypothesis was tested by..." variable → "Several variables were controlled..." significant / significance → "A statistically significant difference..." correlation / correlate → "A strong correlation exists between..."

Nhóm 5: Cause & Effect (Crucial for IELTS Essays)

EXPRESSING CAUSE: due to / owing to / because of + noun phrase as a result of / as a consequence of stem from / arise from / result from contribute to / lead to / give rise to be attributed to / be caused by EXPRESSING EFFECT: result in / lead to / cause / bring about / trigger consequently / as a result / therefore / thus / hence have an impact on / affect / influence Ví dụ Band 7 phrases: "Urbanization has given rise to significant infrastructure challenges." "Increased screen time contributes to deteriorating attention spans." "Deforestation stems from economic pressures in developing nations." "Consequently, biodiversity loss has accelerated dramatically." "Poverty is largely attributed to systemic inequality."

Nhóm 6: Academic Hedging (Sắc thái quan trọng)

STRONG claims (có evidence rõ ràng): "This clearly demonstrates..." / "The evidence conclusively shows..." "It is evident that..." / "There is no doubt that..." MODERATE claims (có evidence nhưng không tuyệt đối): "This suggests that..." / "This appears to indicate..." "The data points to..." / "This would seem to support..." WEAK/HEDGED claims (uncertain): "It could be argued that..." / "It is possible that..." "This may be due to..." / "There is some evidence to suggest..." "Under certain conditions..." / "In some cases..." Tại sao hedging quan trọng trong học thuật: Academic writing values PRECISION over confidence. Overclaiming = bad academic writing (even if you're right) Appropriate hedging = sophisticated writer

Phần 7 — Ngữ Pháp

Grammar For Exams

Ngữ pháp cụ thể được test trong từng chứng chỉ — không phải grammar chung chung.

Grammar cho IELTS Writing (Band 7+ đòi hỏi)

1. COMPLEX SENTENCES — phải có trong band 7 Relative clauses: "Countries that invest in education tend to..." Participle clauses: "Having considered all factors, it can be concluded..." Adverbial clauses: "Although technology offers benefits, it also..." Noun clauses: "The fact that pollution is increasing is alarming." 2. PASSIVE VOICE — học thuật dùng nhiều Active (casual): "Researchers found that..." Passive (formal): "It was found that..." / "X has been linked to Y..." "The study was conducted by..." / "Data were collected from..." Tại sao: passive sounds objective, impersonal — đúng với academic register 3. CONDITIONALS — cho discussions và hypotheticals 2nd conditional: "If the government invested more, inequality would decrease." 3rd conditional: "If stricter policies had been implemented, the crisis could have been avoided." Mixed: "If they had addressed it sooner, we would not be facing this now." 4. CLEFT SENTENCES — emphasis trong writing "What is needed is a comprehensive policy reform." "It is education that holds the key to economic development." "The reason why this is problematic is that..." 5. INVERSION — advanced, band 7-8 "Not only does this harm the environment, but it also..." "Rarely has such rapid change been observed." "Under no circumstances should governments ignore..."

Grammar cho TOEIC Part 5 (High Frequency)

1. WORD FORMS (chiếm ~40% Part 5) Economy (n) / economic (adj) / economical (adj) / economically (adv) / economize (v) Employment (n) / employed (adj) / employer (n) / employee (n) / unemployed (adj) Strategy: blank ở vị trí nào trong câu → xác định loại từ cần điền 2. VERB TENSES (chiếm ~20%) "By the time she arrives, the meeting ___" → will have finished (future perfect) "The report ___ yesterday" → was submitted (simple past passive) "We ___ the project since January" → have been working on (present perfect continuous) 3. PREPOSITIONS + COLLOCATIONS (~15%) responsible FOR / depend ON / result IN / consist OF / comply WITH in accordance with / on behalf of / due to / regardless of apply for a job / comply with regulations / result in failure 4. ARTICLES (~10%) a/an = first mention, general the = specific, known, unique no article = general plural nouns, abstract nouns "The manager approved the report." vs "Managers approve reports." 5. CONJUNCTIONS & TRANSITIONS (~15%) Contrast: although, though, even though, while, whereas, despite, in spite of Cause: because, since, as, due to, owing to Result: therefore, consequently, as a result, thus Addition: furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally

Advanced Grammar: Key Distinctions

Cặp dễ nhầmSự khác biệtVí dụ
although vs despitealthough + clause; despite + noun/gerund"Although it rained..." / "Despite the rain..."
since vs forsince + point in time; for + duration"Since 2020" / "For three years"
much vs manymuch + uncountable; many + countable"much progress" / "many improvements"
fewer vs lessfewer + countable; less + uncountable"fewer people" / "less time"
rise vs raiserise = intransitive; raise = transitive"Prices rose." / "They raised prices."
affect vs effectaffect = verb; effect = noun (usually)"This affects output." / "The effect is..."
which vs thatwhich (non-defining, add info); that (defining, restrict)"The report, which was late,..." / "The report that matters..."
used to vs wouldused to = past habit or state; would = past repeated action onlyBoth: "I used to/would go." But: "I used to be shy." (not would)
Phần 8 — Giao Tiếp

Communication Frameworks

Các framework giao tiếp chuyên nghiệp — dùng cho cả exam speaking và giao tiếp hàng ngày.

PREP — Trả Lời Có Cấu Trúc

Point → "I believe remote work is more productive." Reason → "Because it eliminates commuting time." Example → "When I worked from home, I finished 30% faster." Point → "So yes, remote work boosts productivity."

STAR — Kể Chuyện Chuyên Nghiệp

Situation → Bối cảnh Task → Trách nhiệm của bạn Action → Hành động CỤ THỂ của bạn (dùng "I") Result → Kết quả + số liệu nếu có

ACS — Disagree Lịch Sự

Acknowledge → "I see your point..." Counter → "However, I'd push back on..." State → "My view is that..."

BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

"The bottom line: [conclusion] Here's the context: [background] The reason: [reasoning]"

Filler Phrases — Mua Thời Gian Suy Nghĩ

"That's a great question..." "Let me think about that..." "Off the top of my head..." "Well, it depends on..." "Hmm, how do I put this..." "Could you elaborate on that?" "Just to make sure I understand..." "Are you saying that...?" "Right." / "I see." "That makes sense." "Exactly!" / "Absolutely."
